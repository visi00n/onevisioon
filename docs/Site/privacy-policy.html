One Visioon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

One Visioon is a King James Bible study app focused on daily Scripture reading, saved progress, notes, highlights, and deeper chapter study.

Information stored in version 1.0

  • Reading progress
  • Streaks and activity history
  • Bible notes and highlights
  • Lesson reflections
  • Onboarding choices such as your name, optional email, and selected study path
  • If you use Sign in with Apple, your Apple user identifier and any name or email Apple shares with the app

Information we do not collect in version 1.0

  • Third-party analytics
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Cloud account sync
  • Server-side profile storage

If you choose to enter your name or email in the app, or if you sign in with Apple, that information remains on your device in version 1.0 unless and until future sync or newsletter delivery features are added.

Billing in this build

Bible School billing is temporarily disabled in this test build so the full lesson and quest experience can be reviewed without purchase screens.

External links

One Visioon may include optional links to third-party services such as Discord or Instagram. If you follow those links, your activity is governed by those services and their privacy policies.

Children

One Visioon is not designed specifically for children and is intended for general audiences interested in Bible reading and study.

Changes

If cross-device sync or any server-backed account features are added later, this privacy policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before those changes ship.
